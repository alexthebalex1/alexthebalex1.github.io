How it works: conversion runs entirely in your browser using a WebAssembly build of FFmpeg — your audio is never uploaded to a server. First conversion loads the encoder engine (~30 MB) from a CDN, so it may take a few seconds to start.

Note on "16 bit": AAC is a compressed format and doesn't store fixed-depth PCM samples the way WAV does, so there's no literal "16-bit AAC." This tool instead treats the sample rate (16000 Hz) and channel count (mono) as the two settings you named, and exposes AAC's actual quality control — bitrate — as a separate field, defaulted to 16 kbps to match what you asked for. Raise it if 16 kbps sounds too compressed for your source material.
